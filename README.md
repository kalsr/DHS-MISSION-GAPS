The Homeland Security Mission Gap Analytics Suite, developed by Randy Singh from Kalsnet KNet Consulting Group, brings eight decision support tools together
in one application. Each tool targets a homeland security gap documented by the Government Accountability Office, the Intelligence Community or security analysts. 
The eight modules cover critical infrastructure cyber risk, drone incursions near protected sites, disaster recovery aid, border security metric reporting, major 
acquisition program health, open audit recommendations, deepfake and impersonation attempts, and insider threat and supply chain controls at facilities. 
Each module explains the gap it addresses, scores every record with a clear and repeatable method, sorts the results into plain bands such as Critical, High and 
Low or Green, Yellow and Red, and shows leaders where to act first.
