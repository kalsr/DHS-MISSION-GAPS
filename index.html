DHS-Mission-Gaps-Analytics

DHS-Mission-Gaps-Analytics Explanation: What the application does The Homeland Security Mission Gap Analytics Suite, developed by Randy Singh from Kalsnet KNet Consulting Group, brings eight decision support tools together in one application. Each tool targets a homeland security gap documented by the Government Accountability Office, the Intelligence Community or security analysts. The eight modules cover critical infrastructure cyber risk, drone incursions near protected sites, disaster recovery aid, border security metric reporting, major acquisition program health, open audit recommendations, deepfake and impersonation attempts, and insider threat and supply chain controls at facilities. Each module explains the gap it addresses, scores every record with a clear and repeatable method, sorts the results into plain bands such as Critical, High and Low or Green, Yellow and Red, and shows leaders where to act first. How it works Every module follows the same workflow. Data is loaded and checked, each input is converted into a component scored from zero to one, the components are combined with weights into a score from zero to one hundred, and each record is placed into a band. The cyber module multiplies the likelihood of compromise by the impact on the sector and the population served, then names the single most valuable fix for each facility. The counter drone module scores each sighting on proximity, altitude, loitering time, payloads, night flight and missing Remote ID, then ranks sites as hotspots based on sighting volume over the last thirty days. The disaster aid module matches each community to the recovery programs it appears eligible for, counts the days left in each application window, lists the documents needed and scores overall need. The border metrics module runs completeness, timeliness, consistency and validity checks on all forty three required metrics. The acquisition module uses earned value to forecast each program's final cost and combines that with schedule slip, test results and key performance parameters into a health score with an early breach warning. The recommendations module scores open items on priority, age, overdue status and missing evidence. The deepfake module combines content credentials, callback verification, liveness and behavioral red flags into one risk score with firm override rules. The facility module measures coverage of background checks, vendor assessments and component inspections, together with audit age and unusual badge activity. Data and reporting Every module can generate synthetic data for training, demonstrations and testing, or accept real data uploaded as CSV files. A downloadable template shows the expected columns, and missing values are filled with cautious defaults. Each module has a field dictionary explaining every input and why it matters, along with the formulas written out step by step. Results can be exported as CSV, plain text, Word or PDF reports, and existing reports in any of those formats can be uploaded and viewed inside the application. Tools used The suite is written in Python, with Streamlit providing the interactive web interface and the tabbed layout. Pandas and NumPy handle data cleaning, scoring and synthetic data generation, and Altair draws the charts, heatmaps and trend views. Graphviz renders the flowcharts, and the formulas are displayed with mathematical typesetting. Python docx, pypdf and fpdf2 create and read Word and PDF files. Real world benefits The suite helps homeland security leaders put limited people and money where risk is highest, instead of spreading them evenly or reacting after an incident. Cyber and facility teams get specific first actions rather than long reports. Scattered drone sightings become evidence that can support policy and enforcement decisions. Small communities without grant staff get clearer guidance and are less likely to miss aid deadlines. Border metric reports reach Congress complete and with fewer data errors, while acquisition overruns and long running audit weaknesses are spotted and addressed sooner. Fraud and false directives that rely on deepfakes become harder to carry out. Because every score and formula is visible, decisions are consistent, explainable and ready for oversight. And because all eight modules share one design, new use cases can be added quickly without rebuilding the application.
